The Scholar's Atelier

An executive office that still feels like a classroom: a library you can climb, a desk ruled like a notebook, a wall to think on, and a bed for the nights the work runs late.

68 seconds · 1280 × 720 · design model built from the Week 3 drawings Download MP4
The film

One minute, door to bed

The walk starts at door D01 and moves past the Ledger desk and its north light, along the 3.9 m iroko library with its brass ladder, to the rest zone on the deep-teal south wall. It turns to the Thinking Wall and the tea pantry, stops under the Halo, then stays at the bed while the lighting changes from Study to Reading, Salon and Late night.
