The tour

Four places to stand

Class B15 is a 57.24 m² classroom with a 4.6 m ceiling, two north windows and two doors to the corridor. We kept all of it and furnished it like the study of a collector-scholar: an iroko library you can climb on the east wall, the Ledger desk under the north light, a bed against the deep-teal south wall, and a Thinking Wall to the west.

These panoramas come from the design model built from the Week 3 drawings, lit in the daytime Study scene. The small plan in the corner shows where you are standing and which way you are facing.
